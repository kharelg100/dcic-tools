DCIC Evidence Tools

Interactive companions to the research briefs prepared for the City of Fort Worth Data Center and Infrastructure Commission. Each opens in any web browser; nothing is installed and no data is collected.

Pillar 3 Equity Tool

Who and what are within a mile of each Fort Worth data-center campus: site boundary, one-mile band, roads, city limits, schools, child-care centers, parks, resident count, and the CDC/ATSDR Environmental Justice Index share. Companion to Section 4.3.5 of the Commission report.

Pillar 2 Cooling and Water Tool

Where the water goes in data-center cooling, a calculator with the Pillar 2 brief's numbers, and what Fort Worth's closed-loop rule does and does not settle. Companion to Section 4.2 of the Commission report.

Pillar 3 Scenario Guide

Five questions the land-use evidence can help answer: setback distances, "ambient plus 5" noise limits, generator testing versus emergencies, the thermal footprint, and who is nearby. Companion to Section 4.3 of the Commission report.

Prepared by Dr. Gehendra Kharel, STREAM Lab, Texas Christian University, for the Fort Worth Data Center and Infrastructure Commission, October 2026. The tools describe evidence; they do not recommend for or against data centers and are not a legal opinion. Dated copies of each release are in the versions folder of the repository.
