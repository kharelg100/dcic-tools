DCIC Evidence Tools

Interactive companion to the research briefs prepared for the City of Fort Worth Data Center and Infrastructure Commission. It opens in any web browser; nothing is installed and no data is collected.

Pillar 3 Equity Tool

Who and what are within a mile of each Fort Worth data-center campus: site boundary, one-mile band, roads, city limits, schools, child-care centers, parks, resident count, and the CDC/ATSDR Environmental Justice Index share. Companion to Section 4.3.5 of the Commission report.

Prepared by Dr. Gehendra Kharel, STREAM Lab, Texas Christian University, for the Fort Worth Data Center and Infrastructure Commission, October 2026. The tool describes evidence; it does not recommend for or against data centers and is not a legal opinion. Dated copies of each release are in the versions folder of the repository.
